# program test: TestProgram_AHoldReachesTheFanOut
#
# The fan-out block while a hold lands, at 120 columns
# (docs/capabilities/subagents.md#a-hold-reaches-the-whole-fan-out).
#
# What the capture is for: the word a parked lane says, and the tally over it.
# A held child is not an idle one — idle is a turn that was cancelled and
# wants steering, and this is a fan-out the reader stopped on purpose — so the
# lane says `⏸ held` in the field on the right and keeps ◇ in the glyph
# column, the way every lane state does. The hold is the session's and reaches
# every child, but each child stops at its own round boundary: the two writers
# here park ten-odd seconds apart, and the header counts the parks as they
# land.
#
# The pacing is the endpoint's rather than a sleep in this file: one writer
# answers every few seconds and parks almost at once, the other holds its
# round open and parks when it comes back.
setup printf 'package agent\n\nfunc loop() {\n    for round := 0; round < limit; round++ {\n        step()\n    }\n}\n' > loop.go
snap 01-start "Some things worth doing first"
keys "survey the round accounting" Enter
# Spawning is gated, and the round asked for both children together, so it is
# one card with a row per child.
snap 02-spawn-card "Spawn 2 writers"
keys y
snap 03-lanes "2 agents"
# The hold, pressed while the session's own turn is still going. The children
# are asked with it, because it was asked of the session.
keys C-p
# The first park: one lane has reached its own boundary and says so, and the
# other has not got there yet — which is the whole reason the header counts
# rather than states.
snap 04-first-park "1 held · 1 running" "⏸ held"
# The second, ten-odd seconds later. The tally is what says the hold has
# landed whole.
snap 05-both-parked "2 held" "⏸ held"
# One press lets them all go, because the hold was asked of the session and
# letting them out one at a time would be a list nobody could keep.
keys C-p
snap 06-released "working"
