# A turn waiting on the model, through the built binary: a step of two
# commands, each asked on the approval card, and then a request the endpoint
# answers with headers and nothing else. Read the cells against the `Waiting`
# artboard and the turn-status and live-card-words goldens.
#
# While the second command waits on the reader, the step's card says
# `waiting for you` with no figure beside it: its clock has stopped on the
# reader (docs/interface/surfaces.md#the-step). Once the last command has
# returned the card is finished — its outcome and the commands' own seconds,
# not `running` and the wall's minutes — and the frame's top rail says what
# the turn is waiting on instead: `waiting…`, the model's clock since the
# request, and `silent — nothing arrived`, never `acting…` or `thinking…`
# (docs/interface/surfaces.md#the-input-frame). The idle deadline is forty
# seconds here, so ten seconds into the silence the clause becomes the retry
# coming, `silent — retry in`.

sleep 1
