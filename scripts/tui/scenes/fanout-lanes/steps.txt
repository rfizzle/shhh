# The fan-out card in the transcript at 120 columns, driven to the state the
# Rows artboard's sub-agents screen draws: three children, one still going,
# one finished and one waiting on an answer.
#
# What the capture is for is the card's grammar rather than its plumbing. The
# header is the spawn's receipt, `◇ spawned 3 agents`, and the sentence that
# titled the spawn is its body. Every child is a row of the card's footer and
# keeps ◇ in the glyph column whatever state it is in — the column says what
# the child is — with its name in a slot of its own, what it is doing after
# it, and how it stands on the right: a meter, `✓ done`. The child waiting on
# you leads its row with `blocked` and is the one row with a key.
#
# The session asks to run nothing of its own. That is not tidiness: a card of
# the session's is the one an answer takes first, so leaving it out is what
# leaves the children's routed requests as the cards the reader lets through,
# one at a time and in the order their queues stand them in.
setup printf 'package agent\n\nfunc loop() {\n    for round := 0; round < limit; round++ {\n        step()\n    }\n}\n' > loop.go
snap 01-start "Some things worth doing first"
keys "survey the round accounting" Enter
# Spawning is gated, so a card stands between the sentence and the children —
# one card with a row per child, because the round asked for them together and
# three cards would be one decision put three times.
#
# The card lands a moment after the enter that sent the sentence, so it takes
# the keyboard with a grace window open, and the answer waits for the window
# to close rather than racing it. On a fast host the snap below is taken
# inside the window, and a `y` pressed straight after it is discarded.
snap 02-spawn-card "Start 3 writers"
sleep 1
keys y
sleep 4
# Three rows as the card opens, before anything has been asked of the
# reader: the spawn's receipt as the header, the sentence that titled it as
# the body, and the kind glyph on every row with the state on the right.
snap 03-lanes "spawned 3 agents" "Fanning three writers over the round accounting."
sleep 4
# Every child then asks to run a command. Each request is routed to this
# session to be answered, and a routed request nobody has answered is what
# the card draws as blocked, on the child's own row with the manager's key.
keys Escape
sleep 2
snap 04-blocked "blocked · " "[f12] agents"
# The first child let through runs its command and then asks the model again,
# and the model keeps it waiting: that lane is the one still working.
keys C-Space
sleep 2
keys y
sleep 5
# The second child let through is answered at once, so its lane is the one
# that has finished.
keys C-Space
sleep 2
keys y
sleep 5
# The third is never answered, and the card standing over the draft is left
# standing: that lane is the one waiting on you.
keys Escape
sleep 2
# Waited for by the finished lane, the last of the three to arrive: `blocked`
# has been on the screen since the step before. The lane still going counts
# the plan its writer opened with — the step it marked of the steps it named
# — where a lane with no plan spins. The step it is on follows its task, and
# the lane's spend gives way before the step does, so at this width the step
# is still on the row.
snap 05-three-states "✓ done" "blocked · " "1 of 3 steps ·" "· Run the count"
# The finished lane carries what the child actually wrote, folded: the count
# of lines behind the fold, and on the row above it the report's first line
# and the assumptions it stated instead of asking, its costs giving way
# before them.
#
# From 130 columns the inspector rail takes its columns out of the pane, and
# the row gives up the end of the count first: `· 2 assump…`. The comparison
# is the part both sides draw, so the scene can be driven wide to read the
# rail.
snap 06-report-fold "report ·" "▸ report · 6 lines" "· 2 assump"
# Reading mode, and the key that opens every other fold in the transcript
# opens this one. The report is read where the child ran rather than by
# attaching to a child nobody thinks to attach to.
#
# The cursor is walked to the card rather than counted to it: [k] stops at
# the first row it can stand on, which is the card — the sentence that titled
# the spawn is its body and no stop of its own — whatever else the turn has
# landed by then.
keys C-o
sleep 2
keys k k k k k k k k k k
sleep 1
keys Enter
sleep 2
snap 07-report-open "▾ report · 6 lines" "## Assumptions"
