# A stall on a narrow terminal: the scripted endpoint refuses two requests
# with a 503, so the session draws the failure card stalled and the retry wait

# offer, whole at 60 columns — waits the stall out twice, and the third

# each snap waits on the attempt only that wait carries. `[m]` is not on the
# block: the scripted model has no price, and the session offers no cheaper
# model it cannot name one for.
#
# Then a failure that is not waited out: a 400 ends the next turn on its card
# and a total that says it failed, and the card, with nothing selected, is the
# one the handover reaches: it says so — `[r] retry the last failure` — and
# draws its letters grey beside the handover. The handover is pressed with
# half a sentence in the draft and gives the card the keyboard, its `r` asks
# the turn again and leaves the retry's line where the prompt would stand, the
# keyboard goes back to the draft, and the sentence is still there. The
# retry ends on a total of its own under its line, counting the one read it
# made, while the failed attempt's total above the line keeps its two
# (docs/interface/surfaces.md#the-turns-close).
