#
# The sentence and the two spawns are one reply, so the transcript titles the
# step with the sentence rather than closing a turn before the calls were
# asked for.

# The session's own turn closes here, and the close is where the notebook is
# first reported: neither child's report is shown in full, so the line naming
# what they wrote down is the only thing that says anything was written.
#
# It waits first. The close counts the notebook as it stands when the turn
# ends, and the second writer is still going when the first one's report
# comes back — a session that answered at once would close over half a
# fan-out and report half of what it left. The hold is long: the second
# writer is done in about three seconds on a quiet machine, but on a loaded
# runner its two round trips have taken eight, and a hold that only just
# outlasted the quiet case closed over one note where there were two.
