# The shared notebook at 120 columns: two children write into it, the turn's
# close says so, and the screen the person reads it on is opened, walked and
# corrected.
#
# What the capture is for is the two things the notebook could not do before
# it had a screen. The first is being read at all: the close names what the
# fan-out wrote and nothing more, and a store agents write to without asking
# has to be one the person can open. The second is being corrected — the one
# key here that takes something out of the notebook, behind the confirm every
# destructive key in the product is behind.
#
# The list is grouped under the agent that signed each note, which is the
# whole reason this is a screen rather than a printed list: a fan-out's notes
# arrive interleaved, and who found what is the question somebody asks their
# own session.
setup printf 'package agent\n\nfunc loop() {\n    for round := 0; round < limit; round++ {\n        step()\n    }\n}\n' > loop.go
snap 01-start "Some things worth doing first"
keys "survey the round accounting" Enter
# Spawning is a decision, and a round that asked for two children is one card
# with a row per child: one key starts the pair.
snap 02-spawn-card "Spawn 2 writers"
keys y
sleep 10
# The close of the session's own turn: the line naming what the children left
# in the notebook, with no rail and nothing in the glyph column, because a
# reading of what the session already holds changed nothing about the machine.
snap 03-close "2 notes from writer-1, writer-2"
keys "/notes" Enter
# The screen: the notes under the agents that signed them, the one the
# pointer is on beside the list, and the key row at the foot.
snap 04-screen "2 notes · 2 agents"
# The pointer walks to the other author's note, which is what the grouping is
# for: the reader moves between authors, not between arrivals.
keys k
sleep 1
snap 05-pointed "The counter is read at the top of the loop"
# The one key that takes something out asks first, and names what it would
# take rather than saying "this note".
keys d
snap 06-confirm "Drop \"The counter is read at the top of the loop\"?"
keys y
# And the screen stays up over the notebook it just corrected: the reader is
# walking a list and fixing it, and leaving after each correction would take
# the list away.
snap 07-dropped "Dropped note"
