# The SPEND block is a door to the session's whole bill
# (docs/interface/surfaces.md#the-inspector-rail), and the screen behind it is
# one of the supporting screens (docs/interface/surfaces.md#the-supporting-screens).
# One plain turn on the session's model, then a turn in auto whose call the
# classifier judges on the small model the launch line names, so the bill has
# two models and two turns. A click is the terminal's own SGR mouse report,
# sent as literal bytes: a press then a release in one cell, column and row
# counted from 1. At 130 columns the rail's first column is 85 and, after the
# second turn, the SPEND heading is on row 10. The rail is not drawn below 130
# columns, which is why the scene is driven there.
#
# What the capture is for: at 04 the rail's SPEND block has a row per model
# and the session total; at 05 a click on the heading has put the screen over
# the pane and the rail, the total first with the whole bill by kind of
# request beside it, both models under `by model` and both turns under `by
# turn`; at 06 a click on the same cell has put the rail back, and the
# half-typed draft is still in the box; at 07 /stats, the key twin, opens the
# same screen; at 08 the pointer is on turn 2 and offers to open it; at 09
# enter has opened turn 2 on the turns screen; at 10 esc is back at the prompt.
