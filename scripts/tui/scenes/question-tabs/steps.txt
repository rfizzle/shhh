# Several questions in one call are one card of tabs with a submit at the end,
# and the marked row's long form is behind the full-view key
# (docs/interface/surfaces.md#the-question-card).
#
# What a golden cannot say and this can: that the arrows reach the strip
# rather than the list under it, that the full view takes the whole screen and
# gives it back with the question still waiting, and that the card is drawn in
# the panel the register placed it in rather than over the transcript.
snap 01-start "Some things worth doing first"
keys "which store should the cache use" Enter
snap 02-reply "I have three forks I cannot settle on my own"
keys "go on then" Enter
# The card arrives over an empty draft holding the keyboard, but nobody handed
# it over, so its bare letters are not live yet: the handover chord is what
# makes the strip's arrows and the full-view key keys at all.
snap 03-card "Which store should the cache use?"
keys C-Space
# The strip: the tab the keyboard is on, the two still to answer, the submit
# last, and the tail that says in words what the marks say in glyphs. `1 of 3`
# is the reading a terminal with one colour is left with.
snap 04-strip "1 of 3"
# The long form of the marked row, on the screen the dry run already takes.
# It answers nothing.
keys d
snap 05-long "in the checkout already"
keys Escape
snap 06-back "1 of 3"
# Answering steps to the next question still open, so a reader working through
# three of them presses no movement key at all. The mark on the first turns and
# the word follows it.
keys Enter
snap 07-answered "2 of 3"
keys y
snap 08-third "3 of 3"
# The arrows are the strip's, which is what the note key not being them is for.
# Right off the last question is the tab that ends the set.
keys Right
snap 09-submit "unanswered"
# And back, to show the strip walks both ways and the answers are still on it.
keys Left
snap 10-walked "3 of 3"
keys Right
# Enter sends every answer at once. The tab nobody answered goes back as
# skipped rather than holding the reader at the card, which is why the model's
# next reply arrives without the free answer ever being typed into.
keys Enter
snap 11-sent "Thank you, that settles all three"
keys C-c
keys C-c
snap 12-exit "that is everything the screen was holding"
