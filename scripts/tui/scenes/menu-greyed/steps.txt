# A menu greys what it cannot offer and says why
# (docs/interface/surfaces.md#the-completion-menu). While the turn runs, both
# menus that list commands keep /compact — behind the ⊘ that says it is out of
# reach, beside the reason it is — rather than dropping it and leaving the
# reader who typed /comp with an empty list and no way to tell a command that
# is waiting from one this build does not have.
snap 01-start "Some things worth doing first"
keys "hold the turn open" Enter
snap 02-card "sleep 15"
sleep 1
keys y
# The command is a wait, so the turn stays open under everything below it and
# the draft keeps the keyboard the whole time.
snap 03-running "running…"
keys "/comp"
snap 04-completion "idle only"
# The palette is the same answer through the other door: the chord opens it
# over the draft, with the whole reach counted on its rail and what did not
# fit folded at its foot.
keys C-/
snap 05-palette "matches"
# Esc alone, with the terminal given time to deliver it: an escape written
# into the pty against the keys behind it arrives as one sequence, and the
# palette would still be up under the chord that was meant for the session.
keys Escape
sleep 0.5
# The turn runs out and the menu is rebuilt on the crossing: /compact is
# offered plainly the moment it can be had, without a keystroke to ask.
snap 06-done "The menu is what the reader has while the turn runs"
# The first cancel takes the draft back — /comp is still in it — and the two
# after it are the session's own way out.
keys C-c
sleep 0.3
keys C-c
snap 07-quit-armed "press again to quit"
keys C-c
snap 08-exit "that is everything the screen was holding"
